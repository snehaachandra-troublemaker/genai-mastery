ORDER-TO-CASH (O2C) POLICY

1. ORDER PLACEMENT
Customer orders can be placed via:
- Online portal (preferred)
  The primary channel for orders, providing real-time inventory checks and 
  immediate confirmation.
- Email: orders@company.com
  Secondary channel for bulk orders or specialized requests that require 
  manual review.
- Phone: 1-800-ORDERS (business hours only)
  Available for urgent inquiries or customers who prefer personal assistance 
  during EST business hours.

Order confirmation sent within 1 business hour.

2. CREDIT CHECK
All new customers undergo credit verification:
- Orders <$5K: Automatic approval
  Small orders are processed instantly to maintain high throughput for 
  standard transactions.
- Orders $5K-$50K: Credit team review (same day)
  Mid-sized orders require a quick evaluation of the customer's payment 
  history and creditworthiness.
- Orders >$50K: Management approval required (1-2 days)
  High-value orders undergo a rigorous risk assessment by the treasury or 
  CFO's office.

Existing customers: Annual credit review.

3. ORDER FULFILLMENT
Standard processing time: 2-3 business days
Expedited shipping: +$50 fee, 1 business day

Shipping carriers:
- US domestic: FedEx, UPS
  Reliable ground and air services for all fifty states and territories.
- International: DHL, FedEx International
  Specialized handling for cross-border logistics and customs clearance 
  requirements.

4. INVOICING
Invoices generated upon shipment via email and customer portal.

Invoice includes:
- Order number
  The unique reference ID linking the invoice to the customer's original 
  purchase request.
- Line items with unit prices
  Detailed breakdown of each SKU shipped, including the quantity and the 
  agreed price per unit.
- Shipping charges
  Transparency on logistics costs incurred based on the selected shipping 
  method and destination.
- Tax (if applicable)
  Calculation of state or international taxes based on the shipping 
  address and product type.
- Payment terms
  The specific timeline and methods by which the customer is expected 
  half to settle the balance.

5. PAYMENT TERMS
- Net 30: Standard for approved customers
  Thirty days from the invoice date to complete the payment for established 
  and creditworthy accounts.
- Net 15: For new customers (<6 months relationship)
  Reduced terms for new partnerships to mitigate initial risk while 
  building a transaction history.
- COD: For orders exceeding credit limit
  Cash on Delivery is required if the customer's outstanding balance 
  surpasses their approved credit ceiling.

Accepted payment methods:
- Wire transfer (preferred)
  Direct bank-to-bank transfer for fastest settlement and reconciliation.
- ACH
  Electronic clearing house payments for secure and low-cost transactions.
- Credit card (3% processing fee)
  Convenient payment option subject to a surcharge to cover processing 
  and interchange costs.
- Check (US only)
  Standard physical checks accepted for domestic customers, subject to 
  clearing delays.

6. PAYMENT APPLICATION
Payments applied to oldest invoices first.
  A First-In-First-Out (FIFO) approach to ensure aging receivables are 
  cleared systematically.
Partial payments allocated proportionally across open invoices.
  If the amount does not cover a full invoice, it is spread across 
  outstanding balances to reduce overall exposure.

7. COLLECTIONS PROCESS
Day 0-30: No action (within terms)
  Monitoring period while the invoice remains within its active and 
  current status.
Day 31-45: Automated reminder email
  Gentle notification sent to the customer billing contact regarding 
  overdue balances.
Day 46-60: Collections team calls customer
  Direct personal outreach to resolve potential payment delays or 
  administrative errors.
Day 61-75: Account placed on hold, escalation to CFO
  Suspension of new orders until the balance is settled, with high-level 
  management oversight.
Day 76+: Potential legal action, debt collection agency
  Final measure involving third-party intervention to recover persistent 
  unpaid debts.

8. RETURNS AND CREDITS
Return policy: 30 days from delivery

Return process:
- Contact customer service: support@company.com
  Initiate a return request by providing order details and the reason 
  for the return.
- Obtain RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) number
  A mandatory tracking ID that must be clearly marked on all return 
  shipments for processing.
- Ship back to warehouse address
  Customer is responsible for return shipping costs unless the items 
  are defective or incorrect.
- Credit issued within 5 business days of receipt
  A credit memo or refund will be processed once the items are inspected 
  and restocked.

Restocking fee: 15% for non-defective returns
  A standard charge to cover the administrative and logistical costs of 
  processing non-error returns.

9. ACCOUNT STATEMENTS
Sent monthly to: customer billing contact
  A summary of all transactions, credits, and open balances for the 
  preceding period.
Available 24/7 on customer portal
  Real-time access for customers to download statements and track 
  payment history at their convenience.

Statement inquiries: ar@company.com
  Dedicated support for questions regarding invoice accuracy, 
  payment application, or balance discrepancies.
Response time: 1-2 business days
  The accounts receivable team commits to addressing all statement-related 
  questions promptly.
