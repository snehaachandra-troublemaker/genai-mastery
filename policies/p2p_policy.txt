PROCURE-TO-PAY (P2P) POLICY

1. PURCHASE REQUISITION PROCESS
All purchase requests exceeding $1,000 must be submitted through the procurement 
system with:
- Business justification
  Provide a detailed explanation of why the purchase is necessary and how it 
  aligns with department goals.
- Budget code approval
  Ensure the correct General Ledger (GL) account and cost center are selected 
  to reflect accurate financial reporting.
- Manager sign-off (Director level for >$10K)
  Obtain digital approval from the relevant budget holder; expenditures above 
  $10,000 require higher-level oversight from a Director or VP.

Processing time: 2-3 business days for standard requests.

2. VENDOR ONBOARDING
New vendors must complete:
- W-9 or W-8 form submission
  Collect the appropriate tax identification documentation to ensure compliance 
  with IRS reporting requirements.
- Bank account verification
  Verify banking details through a secure portal or direct contact to prevent 
  fraudlent payment redirections.
- Insurance certificate (COI) for service providers
  Confirm that the vendor maintains adequate liability coverage as specified 
  in the master service agreement.
- Vendor master data entry in SAP
  Create a unique vendor ID and input all relevant contact and payment details 
  into the enterprise resource planning system.

Typical onboarding time: 5-7 business days.

3. PURCHASE PO CREATION
POs are automatically generated after PR approval. Vendor receives PO via email 
with:
- Line item details
  A comprehensive list of products or services including descriptions, 
  quantities, and agreed-upon unit prices.
- Delivery address
  The specific physical location where goods should be shipped or where 
  services are to be rendered.
- Payment terms (Net 30 standard)
  Clarification that payment will be disbursed 30 days after receipt of a 
  valid invoice, unless otherwise negotiated.

4. GOODS RECEIPT
Upon delivery, receiving department must:
- Verify quantity and quality
  Inspect all packages for damage and cross-reference the packing slip against 
  the original purchase order.
- Create goods receipt in SAP within 24 hours
  Log the delivery into the system immediately to trigger the three-way 
  match process for invoice payment.
- Report discrepancies to procurement
  Immediately notify the purchasing team if there is a shortage, overage, or 
  if the items do not meet specified standards.

5. INVOICE PROCESSING
Vendors submit invoices to: ap@company.com

Required information:
- PO number (mandatory)
  All invoices must clearly display the valid 10-digit PO number to facilitate 
  automated processing and matching.
- Invoice date
  The date the invoice was issued, which serves as the starting point for 
  calculating the payment due date.
- Line item details matching PO
  Ensure that descriptions and prices perfectly align with the PO to avoid 
  mismatches and payment delays.

Processing time: 5-10 business days after goods receipt.

6. PAYMENT TERMS
- Net 30: Payment within 30 days of invoice date
  This is the default term for all standard vendors, ensuring steady 
  cash flow management for the company.
- Net 60: For approved vendors only
  Extended terms reserved for high-volume or strategic partners who have 
  signed specific long-term contracts.
- Early payment discount: 2% for payment within 10 days
  An incentive offered to vendors who prefer faster liquidity in exchange 
  for a small reduction in the total invoice amount.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
For invoice disputes:
- Contact procurement team: procurement@company.com
  Send a formal inquiry to the centralized mailbox for tracking and 
  accountability purposes.
- Provide: PO number, invoice number, issue description
  Include all relevant identifiers and a clear explanation of why the 
  invoice is being contested.
- Resolution SLA: 5 business days
  The procurement team aims to investigate and provide a path to 
  resolution within one business week.

8. ESCALATION PROCESS
Unresolved issues after 5 days:
- Escalate to Procurement Manager
  If the initial SLA is missed, the issue is elevated to leadership for 
  immediate intervention.
- CC: finance@company.com
  Keep the finance department informed of any significant delays that 
  might impact financial closing or vendor relations.
- Include full transaction history
  Provide an audit trail of all previous communications and actions taken 
  to resolve the matter.
